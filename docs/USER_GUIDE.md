# FreshKeep — User Guide

FreshKeep helps you eat your food before it goes off. Add groceries with your camera, get a
reminder before they expire, and see how much food and money you've saved.

## 1. Getting started

1. Open **FreshKeep**. Swipe through the three welcome pages (or tap **Skip**).
2. Tap **Get started** and allow notifications so FreshKeep can remind you before food expires.
3. Your pantry is empty at first. Tap **Scan food** or **Add manually**.
   *Tip:* **Settings → Load sample pantry** fills the app with demo items so you can explore.

## 2. Adding food

### Scan with the camera (fastest)
1. Open the **Scan** tab and keep **Food** selected.
2. Tap **Camera** and take a photo of the item (or **Photos** to pick one).
3. FreshKeep recognises the food on your phone and shows the best matches with a confidence bar.
4. Tap the right match. The form opens already filled in — name, category, storage and an
   estimated expiry date. Adjust anything and tap **Save**.
5. Not right? Tap **None of these — add manually**.

### Read the date from a label
1. In **Scan**, switch to **Date label**.
2. Photograph the best-before / use-by date on the packaging.
3. FreshKeep shows the date it found (it ignores the manufacture date). Tap
   **Add item with this date**, type the name and save.
   You can also tap **Read date from label photo** inside the add form.

### Add by hand
Tap **+** on the Pantry screen. Start typing a name — suggestions appear; picking one fills in
the category, storage and expiry estimate. Changing **Stored in** (Fridge / Freezer / Pantry /
Counter) updates the estimate automatically unless you set a date yourself.

## 3. Your pantry

* **Needs attention** at the top shows everything expiring today, soon, or already expired.
  Swipe through the cards and tap one to open it.
* Items are grouped by urgency: **Expired**, **Use today**, **Use soon**, **Fresh**.
  The ring shows how much time is left; the number is days remaining.
* Filter by **Fridge / Counter / Pantry / Freezer**, search by name, or change the sort order
  (⇅ button).
* **Swipe right** on an item → **Eaten**. **Swipe left** → **Wasted** or **Delete**.
* **Touch and hold** an item for a preview and quick actions (Eaten, Freeze it, Wasted, Delete).

## 4. Item details

Tap any item to see its freshness ring, when it expires, how the date was decided (estimated,
read from label or entered by you), a storage tip and recipe ideas.

* **I ate it** — marks it as rescued (with a little celebration 🎉).
* **Freeze** — moves it to the freezer and extends its expiry date.
* **Wasted** — logs it so Insights can show what you tend to throw away.
* **⋯ → Edit / Delete** — change details or remove it without counting it.
* Pinch the photo to zoom.

## 5. Reminders

FreshKeep sends two reminders for each item: a "use soon" reminder (2 days before by default)
and one on the morning it expires. Change the timing in **Settings → Notifications**, or turn
reminders off.

## 6. Insights and recipes

The **Insights** tab shows your **rescue rate**, money saved (LKR), items rescued and wasted,
CO₂e avoided, your zero-waste streak, a 6-week chart and which categories you waste most.
**Use it up** lists recipes that use what's expiring next — tap one for ingredients and a
step-by-step checklist.

## 7. Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Expiry reminders | Turn notifications on/off |
| Remind me N days before | Lead time for the "use soon" reminder |
| Reminder time | Time of day reminders arrive |
| "Use soon" when N days left | When items move into the *Use soon* group |
| Theme | System, Light or Dark |
| Load sample pantry | Adds demo items and history |
| Show welcome screens again | Replays onboarding |
| Erase all data | Permanently deletes all items and history |

## 8. Accessibility

FreshKeep supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type (all text grows with your system text size),
VoiceOver (every control and the freshness ring are labelled) and Reduce Motion. Status is
always shown as text as well as colour.

## 9. Privacy

Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone. Photos and pantry data never leave your device.
There is no account and no internet connection is needed.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Food not recognised | Fill the frame with one item, use good light, or pick **None of these** |
| Date not found | Get closer to the date, avoid glare, keep the text horizontal |
| No reminders | Check Settings → Notifications in FreshKeep and in iOS Settings |
| Camera button disabled | The device has no camera (e.g. Simulator) — use **Photos** |

## 11. FreshKeep Spatial (Apple Vision Pro prototype)

* The main window lists your pantry; pick an item to see details and recipes.
* **Open 3D Fridge** opens a volume with shelves. Look at an item and pinch to see its card;
  pinch and drag an item into the **Cook now** pot to mark it eaten and get a recipe idea.
* **Expiry Timeline** places food around you — the closer it floats, the sooner it expires.
